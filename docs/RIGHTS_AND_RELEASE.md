# Rights, attribution and public release policy

- Bibliographic references and source DOI identifiers are included for attribution and traceability.
- Author-generated derived statistics and source-pair decisions form the public candidate. They are not publisher figure/table reproductions.
- Do **not** upload publisher PDFs, copied full supplementary tables, cached publisher HTML, private internal replay files, passwords, API keys or folders with unrelated research.
- Per-run measurements, endpoint-level extraction files and the original 114 frozen prediction/observation rows are held back until source license / database rights and attribution requirements have been verified.
- The study involves no identified human participants in the released files.
- A licence has NOT yet been assigned. Public visibility does not grant unrestricted reuse. Before final release, confirm with all authors an appropriate code licence (e.g. MIT) and data licence (e.g. CC BY 4.0 **for content the authors have rights to license**, not for third-party source material).
- Figures are original statistical plots; before applying an explicit licence obtain author approval and check source-study attribution.
